Yes. OKX Wallet on-chain swaps (multi-chain) and Binance Alpha campaign points / volume are both covered, each under its own attribution model. We confirm the active campaign band before you start trading.
What DEXs are supported for the on-chain rebate?+
Coverage rotates with active exchange campaigns. We typically support volume rebates across major perpetual DEXs and aggregators that have an affiliate / partner program — contact Telegram @Jack168668 for the current list and rate.
How is on-chain rebate paid?+
Payout cadence is set by the campaign operator, not by us — most wallet / Alpha campaigns batch payouts on their own schedule into your exchange spot wallet in USDT or campaign tokens, and pure on-chain DEX partnerships sometimes settle on-chain in the trading asset. Check the campaign's own terms for its schedule. This is separate from our exchange fee rebate, which settles in real time in the exchange's official backend.
