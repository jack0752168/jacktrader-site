Self-rebind: an account with no trading history

Binance distinguishes accounts that have never traded from accounts that need an application. Do not apply the application volume target to an account without checking whether Binance offers self-rebind. Its on-screen decision is the authority; a third-party guide cannot approve an account.

Recall: an eligible inactive account

Recall has its own account-age and inactivity conditions. It uses the affiliate's current invitation link rather than a code entered on its own. After logging in to the existing account, an eligible user can confirm the binding in the window Binance displays. Do not register another account when the invitation first opens the sign-up page. Read the official Recall conditions and link instructions.

If you need a JackTrader Recall link, ask Jack to confirm the current link. This page does not certify that an older saved invitation link supports Recall.

Application: an account with trading history

For this route, Binance requires completed KYC and account review, no referrer, a master account, and volume below 5,000 USD in the previous 90 days. Abnormal-trading flags and unsupported regions also prevent eligibility. These are application conditions, not the Recall account-age conditions.

Submission starts a 30-day window. The referral relationship is established when eligible volume reaches 150,000 USD during that window. Binance includes spot, futures, convert, margin and master-account copy trading; sub-account volume and zero-fee trading-pair volume are excluded. The bStocks 3x multiplier does not apply. Do not trade more or take additional risk just to reach this target. Read the official Rebind FAQ.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a Binance referral code to my existing account?+
Possibly, if there is no existing referrer. Binance distinguishes self-rebind for accounts without trading history, the Recall Program for eligible inactive accounts, and an application route for accounts with trading history. Check the relevant official route and the options Binance displays for your account.
Does every existing account need 150,000 USD of trading volume?+
No. The 150,000 USD within 30 days requirement belongs to the Affiliate Rebind Application route. Binance separately describes self-rebind for accounts with no trading history and the Recall Program for eligible inactive accounts. Do not assume one route has the conditions of another.
What is the 90-day rule in the Binance Recall Program?+
Recall requires an account older than 90 days, with no previous inviter and no trading or use of Binance products in the past 90 days. Use the current affiliate Recall link, log in to the existing account, and confirm the binding if Binance offers it. Availability depends on the region and account.
Who needs the Affiliate Rebind Application?+
Binance describes the application route for accounts with trading history and no existing referrer. It requires completed KYC and account review, a master account, trading volume below 5,000 USD in the previous 90 days, and the other eligibility checks. The relationship is established after 150,000 USD of eligible volume within 30 days of submitting.
Can I change a referrer that is already attached to my account?+
No. These routes do not replace an established referrer. Do not close your account or create a duplicate account just to try to obtain a different referral benefit. Ask Binance support to confirm your current referral status if you are unsure.
Must I enter the referral code manually?+
An affiliate-generated rebind link can pre-fill its code; Binance also permits entering a valid code manually on the rebind page. The generic page linked here does not contain the JackTrader code in its URL, so check the displayed referrer and enter MPZQWSDC if requested before confirming.
What if I cannot reach the application volume requirement?+
Do not increase trading just to qualify. Submitting an application does not itself establish the relationship. Binance says failed applications can be submitted again with a new eligibility review; cancellation requires customer support. If another official route fits your account, check that route on its own terms.
Does a successful binding guarantee a fixed JackTrader rebate?+
