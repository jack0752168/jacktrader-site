Binance lifetime fee rebate 2026: is it real, and how do you get one?

"Lifetime rebate" is one of the most-searched and most-misunderstood phrases in crypto referrals. The short answer: the rebate really is ongoing — but "lifetime" means bound to your account, not a fixed percentage guaranteed forever. Here's exactly what you get, what you don't, and how to lock it in.

The short version

A Binance fee rebate paid through a referral channel keeps paying for as long as your account stays bound to the same referrer and you keep trading. For spot trading, there is no 30-day or 12-month timer that switches it off — that's the legitimate sense in which it's "lifetime" for spot. What it is not is a contract that locks a specific percentage forever: any quoted rate is reviewed per account, and the dollars you keep move with your VIP tier. Treat "lifetime" as "ongoing for spot, no expiry counter" — not "a guaranteed percentage until the end of time." (Binance exception: referral commissions on futures accounts are limited to 12 months from when the referred user opens their Futures account. This limit is removed if the referrer has passed the 30% referral commission evaluation. Spot rebates are unaffected.) A rebate is not the only recurring credit on the maker side — see market-maker rebate programs for the exchange-side equivalent and how the two interact.

What "lifetime" really refers to

On Binance, the rebate isn't a coupon you redeem once. It's a property of the referral binding on your account. When you register through a referral link, your account is permanently associated with that referrer in Binance's system. From then on, every trading fee you generate produces referral commission on Binance's side, and the channel can pass a share of it back to you — with nothing to re-claim.

That binding doesn't carry an expiry date. So the rebate runs until one of three things happens:

  1. The binding is removed or the account is closed.
  2. You stop trading (no fees = no rebate, obviously).
  3. Platform policy changes the underlying commission structure.

For spot trading, none of those is a timer — and that's why it's fair to call it a lifetime rebate. (Binance exception: futures commissions stop after 12 months unless the referrer holds a 30% or higher commission evaluation.) Either way, it's misleading to read "lifetime" as "a frozen number".

  • Know which tier the channel sits on. On a standard Binance referral, the referrer earns a base 20% commission and can share only 0%, 5% or 10% of it with you; approved Binance spot affiliates earn from 41% up to 50%. See what a crypto sub-broker is.
  • Enable BNB. The BNB discount cuts the gross fee first (25% spot / 10% futures), and the rebate stacks on top — they don't cancel. Details in the Binance fee calculator.

FAQ

Is the Binance lifetime fee rebate real?+
Yes, in the sense that the rebate keeps paying for as long as your account stays bound to the same referrer and you keep trading. For spot trading there is no expiry counter; for futures accounts Binance limits referral commissions to 12 months from when the referred user opens a Futures account (this limit drops if the referrer has passed the 30% commission evaluation). What "lifetime" does not mean is a fixed percentage forever: the rate is reviewed per account and platform policy can change. So it is an ongoing rebate, not a contractual guarantee of a number.
What does 'lifetime' actually mean for a Binance rebate?+
It means the rebate is attached to the referral binding on your account rather than to a one-off promo. Once your account is bound to a channel, every fee you generate keeps producing referral commission on Binance's side, with no need to re-claim; what share of it reaches you depends on the channel's terms. For spot trading it stops only if the binding is removed, the account is closed, or you stop trading. For futures accounts Binance additionally applies a 12-month limit on referral commissions (waived if the referrer has passed the 30% commission evaluation).
Does the rebate percentage stay the same for life?+
No channel can honestly lock a percentage for life. The absolute dollars you get back move with your VIP tier and trading volume, and at very high VIP tiers the rebate-as-percentage can change because Binance's affiliate rules differ for ultra-low-fee accounts. Anyone advertising a guaranteed fixed percentage forever is overstating it — read the terms.
Can I get a lifetime rebate on a Binance account I already have?+
Possibly. Binance operates a one-time official rebind for existing accounts, but the bar is specific: completed KYC, a master account with no current referrer, and 5,000 USD or less of total trading volume in the past 90 days. That 90 days is a lookback on volume, not a countdown from when you registered — account age is irrelevant. Submitting is also not the end: the binding only takes effect once the account trades 150,000 USD within 30 days, with no partial credit below that. If you are ineligible, the existing binding stands — check before assuming either way.
How do I lock in a lifetime Binance fee rebate?+
Register a fresh Binance account through a referral channel — that is the clean route, with no activation volume to hit. An existing account can only be rebound if it passes Binance's eligibility test (KYC done, master account, no current referrer, 5,000 USD or less traded in the past 90 days) and then trades 150,000 USD within 30 days to activate. Either way, keep the binding in place and enable BNB for the stacking discount. Confirm the rebate rate and payout method with the channel one-on-one, then check what you receive against your Binance fee history so you know it is actually being paid.
