Binance lifetime fee rebate 2026: is it real, and how do you get one?

"Lifetime rebate" is not an unconditional promise. Referral binding and commission eligibility are separate: commissions stop at VIP 3 or higher, and futures commissions also have a 12-month limit unless the referrer has passed Binance's 30% commission evaluation or above. A binding does not guarantee a fixed percentage or payments forever.

The short version

A referral binding alone does not guarantee an ongoing Binance fee rebate. Binance pays referral commissions only for Regular or VIP 1–2 users; commissions stop when the referred user reaches VIP 3 or higher. For futures accounts, there is also a 12-month limit from the day the referred user opens their Futures account, removed only if the referrer has passed Binance's 30% referral commission evaluation or above. The VIP 3 cutoff still applies. Any share passed back to a trader depends on the channel's terms, and neither a fixed percentage nor lifetime payments are guaranteed. These limits come from Binance's Referral Pro FAQ. A rebate is not the only recurring credit on the maker side — see market-maker rebate programs for the exchange-side equivalent and how the two interact.

What "lifetime" really refers to

On Binance, referral binding identifies the referrer associated with your account. It does not make every future trading fee commission-eligible. Commissions apply only while the referred user is Regular or VIP 1–2 and stop at VIP 3 or higher. Futures commissions also have the 12-month limit described above, removed only if the referrer has passed Binance's 30% commission evaluation or above. A channel can pass back a share of eligible commissions under its agreed terms.

Continued binding is not the same as continued eligibility. Payments can stop when:

  1. The binding is removed or the account is closed.
  2. You stop trading (no fees = no rebate, obviously).
  3. The referred user reaches VIP 3 or higher.
  4. The 12-month futures limit is reached without the qualifying referral evaluation.
  5. Platform policy changes the underlying commission structure.

The futures time limit does not make spot referral commissions unconditional: the VIP 3 or higher cutoff still applies. Passing the qualifying evaluation removes the 12-month futures limit, not the VIP cutoff. It is misleading to read "lifetime" as either guaranteed duration or a frozen percentage.
