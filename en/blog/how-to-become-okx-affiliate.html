How to become an OKX affiliate: step-by-step (2026)

Searching "become OKX affiliate" turns up the marketing page and a promise of headline commission percentages. What it does not tell you is the part that actually decides your income: which of OKX's referral tiers you land on, the volume and headcount targets you then have to keep hitting, and the one shortcut that skips those targets entirely. This is the plain-English walkthrough — the application steps, the gates, the real numbers, and when joining an existing sub-broker tree beats applying on your own.

Here for what the program pays rather than how to join? The commission numbers, tier tables and sub-broker mechanics all live in our OKX affiliate program breakdown — this page is the step-by-step application guide.

First, know which tier you are applying for

"OKX affiliate" is not one thing. There are four referral structures stacked on top of each other, and your whole income depends on which one you are on. From thinnest to fattest:

  1. Default referral. Any account can copy a referral link from its profile. The person you refer gets a small fee discount; you get a token commission. Fine for sharing with friends, not a business.
  2. Affiliate program. Application-based. Once approved you earn a percentage of every referred trader's fees — published as a 30% default that OKX reassesses monthly. The number you actually get scales with your tree's total fee output.
  3. Sub-broker / institutional channel. The tier above standard affiliate. Custom rebate band, a per-trade reconciliation API, a dedicated OKX BD contact, and a pass-through ceiling that reaches up to 40% on futures fees. Onboarded individually after a volume plus KYB review.
  4. Market-maker program. A negotiated maker-rebate band for genuine quoting desks — sits at the exchange-fee layer, not the referral layer, and is a separate application entirely.

Most people who type "how to become an OKX affiliate" want tier 2 or 3. The full mechanics of how those tiers price out are in our OKX sub-broker vs affiliate breakdown; this guide is about getting onto them.

The standard OKX affiliate application, step by step

  1. Open and verify an OKX account. You need a fully KYC-verified account before you can apply. An unverified account cannot hold an affiliate relationship.
  2. Apply through the OKX affiliate portal. OKX runs a dedicated affiliate sign-up form (separate from the in-app "invite friends" link). You describe your channel — a trading community, a content channel, a website, a Telegram group.
  3. Pass the review. Approval is discretionary. OKX is looking for reach: an audience or a community of traders it can plausibly acquire through you. Blank applications with no channel are routinely declined.
  4. Get your links and dashboard. Once approved you receive referral links and an affiliate dashboard that reports clicks, sign-ups and commission. Your commission band is set by — and keeps moving with — your tree's fee output.
  5. Build the back end yourself. If you intend to pass part of the commission back to traders as a rebate, you now have to build the reconciliation, the per-trade ledger and the payout pipeline — most independent affiliates end up batching those payouts on some cycle of their own, because doing it per trade by hand does not scale. This is the part nobody mentions, and it is most of the work. It is also the part that disappears if you join an existing tier instead: settlement then happens in the exchange's own back office, in real time, where the partner can audit it without going through anyone.

The gate everyone hits: the ongoing targets

Getting approved is the easy part. Keeping a good rate is where the standard affiliate program bites. Your commission band is tied to how much fee volume your referred traders generate — see how the OKX affiliate commission tiers set the 0–40% band for the exact mechanics — which means:

  • A headcount expectation. A handful of low-volume referrals will not hold you in a high band. OKX rewards trees that bring real, active traders.
  • A volume expectation. Quiet months can move you down a band. The rate is not fixed for life; it tracks recent output.
  • A retention problem. If your traders churn, your band slides with them. You are effectively running an acquisition and retention business, not just collecting a percentage.

This is the same structure on Binance, by the way — the affiliate band there also tracks referred-volume, which is why the same shortcut applies on both. (For the Binance side, see how the Binance affiliate commission rate really works.)

Worked example: the cost of the targets

Take a trader doing $20M / month in USDT-perpetual notional, 70% maker / 30% taker, sitting at OKX VIP 2 (maker 0.015%, taker 0.036%):

  • Maker = $14M × 0.015% = $2,100
  • Taker = $6M × 0.036% = $2,160
  • Gross monthly fees ≈ $4,260
  • At a sub-broker tree pass-through of 40%: ≈ $1,704 / month back in USDT — about $20,448 / year.
  • At a thin self-applied affiliate band of 20%: ≈ $852 / month — about $10,224 / year.

Same trades, same account, ~$10,000 a year of difference — and the higher number is the one with no ongoing target to defend. That gap, multiplied across a community, is exactly why sub-broker channels exist.

FAQ

What are the requirements to become an OKX affiliate?+
A verified OKX account, completed KYC, and an application through the OKX affiliate portal. Approval is discretionary: OKX looks at your audience, content channel or trading-community reach. There is no public minimum follower count, but empty applications with no channel are commonly rejected. The sub-broker tier above it additionally expects a credible, sustained trading-volume profile or an existing community of active traders.
Do OKX affiliates have to hit headcount or volume targets to keep their rate?+
On the standard affiliate program, yes — your commission band is tied to how much fee volume your referred traders generate, and inactive periods can move you down a band. That ongoing target is the main reason individuals join an established sub-broker tree instead: the tree already clears OKX's volume thresholds, so a trader binding into it gets the high pass-through rate without personally carrying a headcount or monthly-volume quota.
How long does OKX affiliate or sub-broker approval take?+
A standard affiliate application is usually reviewed within a few business days. A direct sub-broker or institutional channel negotiated with OKX is a corporate onboarding — contract, entity KYB and reporting setup — and typically takes two to six weeks. Joining an existing sub-broker tree (for example through JackTrader) is same-day once your OKX email is confirmed, because the tree itself is already onboarded.
What commission rate does an OKX affiliate actually earn?+
OKX's help centre publishes a default commission rate of 30% as the gross share paid to the referrer, applied across spot, futures, options and CeDeFi rather than split per product, and reassessed monthly. The real number scales with your downstream tree's total fee output, and whatever an affiliate site hands back to traders has to come out of that share after its own operating margin — each site sets and publishes that figure itself. A sub-broker channel starts from a larger gross share, which is why the pass-through ceiling reaches up to 40% — a maximum reviewed per account, not a guarantee.
Can I become an OKX affiliate without an audience or trading volume?+
You can apply, but a standard affiliate approval with no channel and no volume tends to land you on a thin commission band, and you would still have to build the dashboard, reconciliation and payout pipeline yourself. The pragmatic route for a trader without an audience is to bind into an existing sub-broker tree and simply receive the high rebate on your own trading, rather than run a referral business from scratch.
Is becoming an OKX sub-broker different from the affiliate program?+
Yes. The affiliate program is application-based and open to most users; the sub-broker or institutional channel is the tier above it, onboarded individually after a volume and KYB review, with a custom rebate band and extras like a per-trade reconciliation API and a dedicated OKX BD contact. Sub-brokers can pass up to 40% of futures fees to traders, where a standard affiliate is working from OKX's published 30% default share.
Is the 'OKEx affiliate' program the same as the OKX affiliate program?+
Yes — OKEx rebranded to OKX in early 2022, so 'OKEx affiliate' and 'OKX affiliate' refer to the same program; only the name changed. If you still see the older OKEx spelling on third-party sites or in search results, it points to today's OKX affiliate and sub-broker structure. The requirements, commission tiers and the up-to-40% sub-broker pass-through described above all apply under the current OKX brand — there is no separate legacy OKEx program to apply to.
