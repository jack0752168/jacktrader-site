How much do OKX affiliates actually make in 2026?

"Up to 40% commission" tells you the ceiling on a percentage — it tells you nothing about the dollars that land in your account. OKX affiliate income is not a salary or a fixed rate; it is a share of the trading fees your referred traders generate, and it scales with exactly one thing: their volume. This is the income math nobody puts on the sign-up page — what a channel really keeps per dollar of referred volume, worked monthly earnings across realistic scenarios, why the self-serve and sub-broker paths pay differently, and the margin trade-off that decides your take-home.

Where the money actually comes from

Every dollar an OKX affiliate earns starts as a trading fee already paid by a referred trader. OKX charges the fee, then shares a slice of it upstream to the affiliate or sub-broker who introduced that trader. Nothing is created — you are paid a cut of costs the trader was going to pay anyway. That single fact governs everything about the earnings: no referred volume means no income, however many sign-ups you have.

OKX publishes a default commission rate of 30% of referred traders' fees, applied across spot, futures, options and CeDeFi rather than split per product, and reassessed monthly on your invitees' volume. Most referred volume in practice is futures, so the futures side dominates real earnings. If you are still mapping how that band is decided in the first place, the mechanics live in our OKX affiliate commission tiers breakdown; this article is strictly about the dollars that fall out of it.

The margin trade-off nobody mentions

If you run your own channel, there is a second lever on your income: how much of the gross commission you keep versus pass back to your traders as a rebate. Keep it all and each trader is worth more to you per month — but a stingy pass-through drives volume away, so your total shrinks. Pass most of it through and you earn less per trader but attract and retain far more volume. The channels that actually scale run a thin margin on a large, sticky base rather than a fat margin on a handful of traders. The full pricing logic of that pass-through gap is laid out in our OKX sub-broker vs affiliate breakdown, and the same arithmetic across exchanges appears in the highest-rebate exchange comparison for 2026.

What actually decides your number

  • Active referred volume, above all. Income tracks the 30-day trading volume of active accounts under you. A large list of dormant sign-ups pays nothing; one active $50M/month desk pays a lot.
  • Your band. Top-of-band vs mid-ladder is a 2× difference on the same volume. This is the single biggest reason to be — or bind under — a reviewed sub-broker.
  • Product mix. The rate is the same across products, so what changes your income is which products actually generate fees — a futures desk pays far more fee volume than a spot-heavy community on the same notional.
  • Retention. Because the window rolls, churn shows up in next month's pay. Stable, active traders compound; a spike of one-off sign-ups does not.
  • Your margin policy. If you operate a channel, the pass-through you offer sets whether volume comes and stays.

How to maximise OKX affiliate earnings

  1. Chase volume, not headcount. One grid-bot or HFT operator can out-earn a thousand casual referrals. Target the traders whose fees are large enough to matter — see crypto trading bot fees for who those are.
  2. Secure a top band. Get onto a reviewed sub-broker contract or bind under one, so every dollar of volume keeps the higher rate instead of a rolling mid-ladder figure.
  3. Run a generous pass-through. A thin margin on sticky volume beats a fat margin that traders leave. Retention is the compounding engine.
  4. Confirm real-time, trackable settlement. You want per-trade visibility through the official back end, not an opaque monthly lump. It is also how you prove your income to yourself.
  5. Stay single-tier. Earn on the traders you directly refer. No downline, no recruiting-other-referrers scheme — that is neither how OKX pays nor something worth your reputation.

FAQ

How much do OKX affiliates make in 2026?+
It scales entirely with the trading volume of the people you refer, not with a fixed salary. OKX pays affiliates a commission out of the fees their referred traders generate — a published default of 30%, applied across spot, futures, options and CeDeFi rather than split per product, and reassessed monthly on your invitees' volume. As a rough guide, a channel earning a top futures band keeps roughly 0.015% to 0.020% of every dollar of referred futures volume. So $10M of monthly referred futures volume produces on the order of $1,500 to $2,000 a month gross, and $100M produces $15,000 to $20,000. There is no floor and no ceiling from OKX itself; a dormant month pays nothing, and the numbers are illustrative, not a guarantee.
What is the difference between self-serve affiliate and sub-broker earnings?+
A self-serve affiliate sits on a rolling, volume-tiered band that is recalculated every 30 days, so a quiet month drops the rate. A reviewed sub-broker negotiates a fixed, higher band up front and holds it for the contract term. On the same referred volume, a sub-broker's fixed top-of-band contract typically earns more per dollar than a self-serve affiliate mid-ladder, and the income is more predictable because it does not re-qualify monthly.
Do I need a minimum trading volume to earn as an OKX affiliate?+
You do not need your own trading volume to earn — you earn from referred traders' volume. Self-serve affiliates can apply with no threshold, but their commission band climbs only as their referred volume grows. Binding under an established sub-broker channel is the way to access the top pass-through band without personally clearing a volume quota, because the channel already passed OKX's review.
How long does it take to earn meaningful money as an OKX affiliate?+
Because commission is paid only on realised trading fees, income starts the month your referred traders actually trade — there is no upfront bonus. Building a base of active traders usually takes a few months; a single high-volume bot operator or a small active community can move the number faster than a large list of dormant sign-ups. The rolling 30-day window means earnings track your active referred volume closely, up and down.
Is OKX affiliate income single-tier or multi-level?+
A legitimate OKX affiliate or sub-broker earns a single-tier commission on the traders they directly refer. You are not paid for recruiting other affiliates, and there is no downline or multi-level structure. Any pitch that pays you for signing up other referrers is not how the OKX program works and should be avoided.
