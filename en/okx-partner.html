What's specific to OKX

OKX prices one rate across every product — so headcount, not product mix, moves your number

This is the part that genuinely differs from Binance, and it changes which audiences are worth bringing here.

OKX's help centre states a default commission rate of 30% of the trading fees a referred trader pays, and states it applies across spot, futures, options and CeDeFi — one rate, rather than a different economics per product. It also describes the rate being re-evaluated periodically against referred volume and active referred headcount. OKX does not publish a fixed ceiling in its help centre, so treat any single headline percentage you see quoted elsewhere with suspicion, including ours.

The practical consequence is easy to miss: on OKX a spot-only community and a futures-heavy desk are paid the same share of whatever fees they generate. Your product mix is rate-neutral. What actually moves the number is total fee output and how many referred accounts stay active — which is why a broad, steady audience can beat a small high-leverage one here, and why the churn on your referred accounts matters more than their favourite instrument. On Binance the opposite is true; we lay that out on the Binance partner page, and side by side in Binance vs OKX affiliate programs.

The second door most people never see. OKX runs a self-serve affiliate sign-up and a separately reviewed broker / sub-broker track. They are not the same product, and OKX does not publish the review criteria or the negotiated bands for the second one — if you want to go that route directly, ask OKX, not a rebate site. Joining under an existing sub-broker tier is simply the third option: no review to clear, and you are paid from 35% — a floor, not a ceiling — settled in real time in the exchange's own back office, where you can audit it yourself. FAQ

OKX partner program — FAQ

Do I have to hit OKX's affiliate volume thresholds to earn the high rate?+
No. If you apply to OKX's affiliate/broker tiers directly, you typically must meet ongoing active-user and trading-volume targets to reach — and keep — the higher commission rates, or you get downgraded. By joining under our existing sub-broker tier, we carry those thresholds: you earn the high split from your very first referral, with no volume quota to hit and no downgrade pressure.
What rate can I actually get — is 35% real?+
Partners start at 35% of the fees their referred users generate — above OKX's 30% affiliate start — and it rises with volume toward 40% for the top tier, with no threshold to unlock it. 35% is a floor set by our tier, not a ceiling; your exact rate is per agreement.
Is this multi-level / MLM?+
No. It is single-level: you earn only on the rebate from traders you directly refer, never from people they go on to refer. There is no downline structure. This keeps it compliant with OKX's official referral rules and avoids multi-level distribution risk.
Do I need to trade a lot myself?+
No. You earn from the rebate generated by the traders you refer. What matters is your ability to reach real traders, not your own trading volume.
Is there a cost or deposit to join?+
Zero cost. No joining fee, no deposit. You just direct people to register.
How and when is the split settled, and can I audit it?+
There's no manual payout: the rebate is settled in real time by OKX's official back-end system and paid automatically in the original currency. You can check every referral's volume and rebate in your own OKX dashboard anytime — it never passes through us.
Can my referred users also get a rebate?+
Yes. You decide how to split the rebate pool between yourself and your users — keep more as commission, or pass more back to win them over.
