About JackTrader

An independent rebate partner, built by an active trader

JackTrader is a Binance rebate and Alpha members group service. We are an independent referral partner — not affiliated with, owned by, or operated by Binance. This page explains exactly who runs it, what we offer, and what we will never do.

Who runs JackTrader

JackTrader is run by Jack, an active Binance futures trader since 2017. The rebate system began as an internal tool to track our own grid-bot and market-making fees on Binance. Once our Binance referral channel had real volume behind it, we opened it to other traders — a $20 cash reward for new users, above-market rebates for larger accounts, and a free Alpha members group for everyone who signs up through us.

We are traders first. Everything on this site is written from the operator's seat, not a marketing desk — which is why the fee guides show the actual math, every VIP tier, and the cases where the rebate matters least as well as most.

What we do

  • $20 cash for new Binance users. Each quarter, the first 15 new Binance users who sign up through a JackTrader invite receive a $20 cash reward. Spots are limited and first-come, first-served — message us on Telegram to confirm how to claim.
  • Premium rebates for $50K+ accounts. For traders with $50,000 or more in funds, we offer an above-market rebate rate. The exact rate is assessed one-on-one — contact us for a quote.
  • Free Alpha members group. Everyone who signs up to Binance through JackTrader can join our Alpha members group for free — a real-time feed aggregating alpha from major secondary-market communities and leading crypto voices. For information only, not investment advice.
  • Fee guides for spot, futures, grid bots, options and on-chain trading — see our Binance rebate and Binance Wallet & on-chain pages.

What we never do

  • We never ask for your password, withdrawal code, API keys, private keys or seed phrases.
  • We never take custody of your funds. The referral is a back-end attribution on Binance's official affiliate ledger — your trading, deposits and withdrawals are unchanged.
  • We don't offer signals, copy-trading, investment advice or fund management. The Alpha members group aggregates market information for reference only, and a rebate lowers your transaction cost; neither changes market risk.
  • Our partner program is strictly one level — you earn on the traders you personally refer, never a multi-level downline.

Is any reward or rebate guaranteed?

No. The $20 new-user reward is limited to the first 15 sign-ups each quarter, first-come, first-served. For $50K+ accounts, the exact rebate rate and how it is paid are confirmed with you one-on-one, and both depend on Binance's official rules and your account status. Anyone promising a fixed rebate regardless of those factors is not describing how the exchange referral system actually works.
