Existing Binance users can rebind too (official rebind route)

Many people assume “I already have a Binance account, so I can’t get a rebate.” Not true — eligible existing accounts can apply to rebind a referral code in under a minute, with no asset access and no API. Note: the referral relationship only becomes effective once you accumulate 150,000 USD in trading volume within 30 days of applying (zero-fee trading volume does not count).

Eligibility (Binance’s own review is the final say):
① KYC verification completed;
② no existing referrer, and the account is a master account (not a sub-account);
③ total trading volume of 5,000 USD or less in the 90 days before applying;
④ not flagged as an abnormal or wash-trading user;
⑤ not resident in a restricted country/region.

1

Submitting is not the same as binding. The relationship only takes effect — and rebates only start — once you trade 150,000 USD within 30 days. Fall short and nothing binds and nothing is paid.
