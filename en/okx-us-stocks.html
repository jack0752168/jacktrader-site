FAQ

OKX tokenized US stocks — common questions

What are OKX xStocks (tokenized US stocks)?+
xStocks are tokenized US stocks on OKX — on-chain tokens that track the price of US-listed shares, settled in stablecoin and tradable in fractional amounts. OKX brings these on-chain via a partnership with Ondo Finance, covering a broad set of US names (around 263 per OKX's announcements). The exact list and rules are per OKX's official pages.
Are xStocks the same as owning the actual shares?+
No. A stock token gives you price exposure, not equity. You do not get shareholder voting rights, dividends and corporate actions are handled per platform rules, and holdings are not covered by securities investor-protection schemes such as SIPC. They suit flexible, crypto-native exposure — not a full replacement for a traditional brokerage account.
