FAQ

Binance US stock tokens — common questions

How are Binance stock tokens different from real stocks?+
Stock tokens track the price of the underlying US stock, are quoted in USDT and support fractional amounts — but you do not own the shares directly. There are no shareholder voting rights, dividends and corporate actions are handled per platform rules, and holdings are not covered by securities investor-protection schemes such as SIPC. They suit traders who want price exposure and flexibility, not a replacement for traditional brokerage ownership.
Who can trade Binance stock tokens?+
Availability depends on your jurisdiction. You need a Binance account with completed KYC and must meet your local regulations. Check availability for your region first — we do not provide advice on circumventing geographic restrictions.
