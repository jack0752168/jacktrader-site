FAQ

Partner program FAQ

Is this multi-level (MLM)?+
No. This is a single-level program — you earn a share of the rebate from the traders you personally refer, never from traders they in turn refer. There is no downline-of-downline. This is what keeps it compliant with Binance and OKX affiliate rules.
Do I need to trade a lot myself?+
No. You earn from the rebate the traders you refer generate — what matters is your ability to influence real traders, not your own volume.
Is there a fee or deposit to join?+
Zero cost. No joining fee, no deposit, no inventory — you just bring people in.
How is the split settled and can I track it?+
Real-time settlement, paid in original currency, with referred-trader volume and rebate statements you can check in your own back end.
I'm already an agent elsewhere — can I switch?+
Let's talk. If your rate is poor, payout slow or there are no statements, we can discuss a higher split and migration based on your size.
Do the traders I refer get a rebate too?+
Yes. The rebate pool can be split between you and your users — you decide how much to pass back vs. keep, to stay competitive.
